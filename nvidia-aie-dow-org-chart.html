Department of War Acquisition Chain

Public sources only · compiled 20 Sep 2026 · unofficial working aid

One page, seven chains. Program Executive Offices are being replaced across every service by Portfolio Acquisition Executives who own a whole mission area from development through sustainment. Contracting officers now report into that chain, which is the part that changes how a deal gets done. Print landscape, or switch to light and screenshot it for a deck.

Same chart as a table, for screen readers and for copying into a deck

Accuracy. Names are as reported in public trade press and official announcements, and many officials are serving in an acting or interim capacity while the transition runs into 2027. Where a role could not be confirmed from an open source the chart says so rather than guessing, and that means unconfirmed, not vacant. The Air Force has seventeen portfolios and the Space Force nine; the ones not consistently published in open sources are shown as a single summary box. Verify on the relevant service acquisition site before a customer meeting.

Sources. Secretary of War acquisition transformation memoranda of 7 November 2025; section 1802 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026; and service announcements and trade reporting through September 2026.
